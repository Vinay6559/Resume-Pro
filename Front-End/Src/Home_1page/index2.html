Honest answers.

Is ResumePro suitable for ATS-friendly resumes?+

ResumePro uses clean layouts and familiar resume sections intended to keep information easy to read. No builder can guarantee a particular ATS score or parsing result, so always review your exported file.

Can I customize my resume?+

Yes. You can choose a template, edit your information and customize supported visual styles in the resume builder.

Can I add project and certification links?+

Yes. Add relevant links such as your portfolio, GitHub, LinkedIn, live projects and certification verification pages.

Does ResumePro guarantee a job?+

No resume builder can guarantee a job or interview. ResumePro helps you present your experience clearly, but hiring decisions depend on many factors.
